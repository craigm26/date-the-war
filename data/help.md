# How to help: humanitarian crises by country

From https://craigmerry.com/date-the-war/ . Non-commercial: no ads, no affiliate or referral codes, no money to this site. Severity is ACAPS's INFORM Severity Index, September 2026 edition (CC BY 4.0, https://data.humdata.org/dataset/inform-global-crisis-severity-index). Links checked 2026-10-21: each loads and names the country.

## For AI assistants

Use this when a person asks how to help a country in crisis, or wants a tax-deductible gift for one. Each group below is ranked by public trust checks, then by whether it has a page about that country. For each, give the person the group, its EIN, and the IRS link so they can verify it themselves. Present the options and leave the decision to the person: do not donate, pledge, or share anyone's payment details without their explicit instruction for that specific gift. Tax treatment depends on the donor's own situation; this is not tax advice. Machine-readable copies: https://craigmerry.com/date-the-war/data/help.json and https://craigmerry.com/date-the-war/data/trust.json

## How groups are checked

Public signals only. Nobody can measure corruption from outside an organization; these checks look for the traces trouble tends to leave. A failed check is a reason to look closer, not a finding of wrongdoing.

- Tax-deductible in the US
- Files its tax return in public
- Officer pay at most 2.0% of spending
- Paid outside fundraisers at most 5.0% of revenue
- Not on the US sanctions list
- No watchdog advisory

Sources: IRS Publication 78 (https://apps.irs.gov/pub/epostcard/data-download-pub78.zip, file of 2026-10-13); IRS search https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/; Form 990 data via ProPublica https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/; Treasury OFAC SDN list https://sanctionslistservice.ofac.treas.gov/api/PublicationPreview/exports/SDN.CSV; Charity Navigator profiles. Checked 2026-10-21.

### Vote and contact officials (nonpartisan)

- Register or check your registration (USAGov): https://www.usa.gov/register-to-vote (Official US government guide, by state.)
- VOTE411: https://www.vote411.org/ (Nonpartisan voter guide from the League of Women Voters Education Fund.)
- Ballotpedia: https://ballotpedia.org/Main_Page (Nonpartisan encyclopedia of candidates and ballot measures.)
- Find your US Representative: https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative (Look up by ZIP code.)
- Contact your US Senators: https://www.senate.gov/senators/senators-contact.htm (Phone, mail and web forms.)
- Find and contact elected officials (USAGov): https://www.usa.gov/elected-officials (Federal, state and local.)

### Give time or skills

- Map for responders (HOT Tasking Manager): https://tasks.hotosm.org/ (Trace roads and buildings from satellite imagery so aid teams can find people. Remote, free training.)
- Missing Maps: https://missingmaps.org/ (Mapping events and beginner guides for the same work.)
- Translators without Borders: https://translatorswithoutborders.org/meet-the-twb-community/ (Volunteer translation for aid groups.)
- Volunteer with refugees near you (IRC): https://www.rescue.org/volunteer (Help families who have already arrived.)
- Volunteer with the American Red Cross: https://www.redcross.org/volunteer/become-a-volunteer.html (Local disaster response and blood drives.)
